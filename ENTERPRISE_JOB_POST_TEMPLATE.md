ENTERPRISE-SCALE MEDIA PRODUCTION & CONTENT DEVELOPMENT ENGAGEMENT

Job Title

Senior Media Production Vendor — Enterprise-Scale Content Delivery (Multi-Format)

Engagement Type

Project-Based with Long-Term Partnership Potential

Work Location

Remote / Global

---

OVERVIEW

We are seeking qualified media production vendors, studios, and creative agencies to support an enterprise-scale online work platform serving global clients across multiple industries. This engagement requires the delivery of professional-grade media assets, video content, audio production, and documentation at volume with strict quality compliance.

The vendor(s) selected will be integrated into a structured workflow designed to serve high-demand clients including mid-market enterprises, Fortune 500 companies, approved vendors, and specialized agencies. Work involves managing multiple concurrent projects with defined SLAs, quality assurance protocols, and brand governance requirements.

This is not a marketplace-based engagement. Selected partners will operate under formal service agreements with guaranteed project flow, transparent rate structures, and performance-based continuation.

---

SCENARIO

A global enterprise client launches a multi-country workforce transformation program through the platform and requires a coordinated release of media and documentation within a fixed eight-week window. The engagement includes: (1) product and lifestyle imagery for localized landing pages, (2) a series of explainer and training videos with subtitles in multiple languages, (3) professional voice-over and audio mastering to regional loudness standards, and (4) executive-ready documentation and slide decks for internal leadership and external partners.

The vendor will receive a structured brief, brand system, and weekly release calendar. Work will be delivered in sprint-based batches with staged reviews (creative, compliance, and executive approval). All assets must meet strict file-format requirements, metadata labeling standards, and version-control documentation. The partnership is expected to scale across multiple regions and product lines with consistent quality, traceable revisions, and measurable SLA performance.

---

SCOPE OF WORK

Image Production & Design

• Development of marketing-focused visual assets including product photography, lifestyle imagery, and branded graphics
• Creation of user interface (UI) components, web banners, email graphics, and digital advertisements
• Preparation of presentation-ready visuals for corporate communications, case studies, and client deliverables
• Compliance with brand guidelines, color specifications, and accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA minimum)

Deliverable Formats:
- PNG (with transparency preservation)
- JPG (optimized for web and print, minimum 300 DPI for print applications)
- SVG (scalable vector graphics for responsive design)
- WEBP (modern web optimization)

File Delivery:
- Editable source files required when applicable (PSD, AI, FIGMA, Adobe XD)
- Organized layer structure with clear naming conventions
- Version control documentation for revision tracking

Video Production & Editing

• Corporate video development including company profiles, training materials, and internal communications
• Explainer video creation with professional motion graphics and animated transitions
• Short-form and long-form video editing for social media, web, and broadcast distribution
• Post-production services including color correction, grading, and professional finishing
• Motion graphics design, animated text sequences, and visual effects as specified

Technical Requirements:
- Professional audio sync and level balancing
- Subtitle/caption integration (SRT, VTT, or burned-in as required)
- Color correction and grading to broadcast standards
- Compliance with platform-specific technical specifications (bitrate, resolution, codec)

Deliverable Formats:
- MP4 (H.264, for universal web distribution)
- MOV (ProRes 422 HQ, for editing and archival)
- Master files with uncompressed audio tracks
- Frame-accurate subtitle files

Audio Production

• Professional voice-over recording, editing, and delivery
• Audio noise reduction, EQ correction, and compression to industry standards
• Audio mastering with loudness compliance (LUFS standards per platform)
• Dialogue editing, timing synchronization, and level balancing
• Music selection, licensing coordination, and audio integration

Deliverable Formats:
- WAV (uncompressed, 24-bit / 48 kHz for master files)
- MP3 (compressed, 320 kbps for web distribution)
- Stem files (separate tracks for dialogue, music, effects when required)

Document & Presentation Development

• Development of enterprise-grade documentation including user guides, process documentation, and technical specifications
• Creation of executive presentations, investor decks, and board-level communications
• Design and assembly of branded templates for recurring internal and client use
• Preparation of white papers, case studies, and thought leadership content
• Report generation with embedded analytics, visualizations, and executive summaries

Deliverable Formats:
- DOCX (Microsoft Word, with tracked changes and version history)
- PPTX (Microsoft PowerPoint, editable animations and master slides)
- Google Workspace native formats (Docs, Sheets, Slides) with sharing and collaboration protocols
- PDF (final, locked format with embedded fonts and metadata)
